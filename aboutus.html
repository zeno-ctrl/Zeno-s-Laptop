About Us

We design and build laptops that push the boundaries of technology. With advanced liquid cooling and premium components, our devices are made for professionals, creators, and gamers who demand performance and style.

Our company was founded with a bold vision: to create the most powerful yet portable laptops in the world. I, Zeno, the Founder & CEO of this brand. With years of passion in technology and innovation, I created this laptop to bring true performance without compromise. And I also created this website.

This is the second-ever liquid cooled laptop in the world. The first one was the Lenovo Legion 9i, a pioneer in introducing liquid cooling to the laptop market. Building on that foundation, our laptop takes liquid cooling to the next level with improved thermal efficiency, thinner design, and unmatched style.

But we didn’t stop there. This is also the first laptop in the world that allows ultra-fast charging and delivers an incredible 36 hours of continuous battery life. No other laptop combines such power, endurance, and innovation in one device.

Our device introduces a new era of mobility — where creators, gamers, and professionals can work and play without worrying about charging every few hours. With intelligent power management and next-gen battery technology, it redefines what a laptop can achieve.

We believe in performance, elegance, and user-focused design. Every laptop we produce is engineered to deliver excellence for those who refuse to settle for ordinary.
